## 4. Notable Roster Moves & Transactions

* **Andy Pages Hand Injury / Alek Thomas Call-Up:** Outfielder Andy Pages sustained a fractured hand after getting hit by a pitch on August 21 and is expected to miss 4–5 weeks. Outfielder **Alek Thomas** was recalled from Triple-A Oklahoma City to take his spot on the active roster.
* **Brock Stewart Reinstated:** Right-handed reliever Brock Stewart was activated from the 15-day Injured List after recovering from right shoulder discomfort.
* **Jonathan Hernández DFA:** RHP Jonathan Hernández was designated for assignment to open up active and 40-man roster flexibility.
* **Tyler Glasnow Nearing Return:** Completed his minor league rehab assignment and is slated to be activated off the 60-day IL to start during the upcoming series against the Atlanta Braves.
